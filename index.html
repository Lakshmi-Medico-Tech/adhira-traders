Fireworks Safety Guidelines
DO's
  • ✔ Always burst crackers in an open area away from buildings & vehicles
  • ✔ Keep a bucket of water or sand nearby at all times
  • ✔ Read and follow instructions on every cracker packet carefully
  • ✔ Light crackers one at a time and move away quickly
  • ✔ Supervise children at all times; let adults handle lighting
  • ✔ Store unused crackers in a cool, dry place away from heat sources
  • ✔ Wear cotton clothes and safety goggles during bursting
DON'Ts
  • ✘ Never burst crackers near hospitals, petrol bunks, or crowded areas
  • ✘ Do not hold crackers in your hand while lighting
  • ✘ Never attempt to re-light a dud cracker
  • ✘ Do not burst crackers near dry leaves, hay, or flammable materials
  • ✘ Never carry crackers in pockets or bags with phones/keys
  • ✘ Do not use crackers if packaging is damaged or wet
